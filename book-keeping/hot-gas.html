Hot Gas
~10–15% of metals  ·  T ~ 106–8 K

Virialized, X-ray-emitting gas spanning coronal halo gas around individual galaxies (T ~ 106–8 K) up to the hot intracluster and intragroup medium (T > 107–8 K). This phase holds a comparable metal share to stars at low redshift, in massive collapsed structures rather than diffuse gas.
