Ionized Gas
~4% of metals at z < 1  ·  log NH I ≲ 17.2

The diffuse, highly ionized end of the H I column density sequence — sub-Lyman-limit systems and partial Lyman-limit systems tracing photoionized circumgalactic gas. Its fractional share of the global metal budget has declined from z ~ 3 to z < 1 even as its absolute metal density has grown.
