Warm-Hot IGM
T ~ 105–7 K  ·  the "missing baryons" phase

Shock-heated intergalactic gas in cosmic-web filaments, thought to host a large share of the Universe's baryons at low redshift. It is difficult to observe directly, traced mainly through highly ionized metal species (e.g. O VI, O VII) and soft X-ray absorption/emission rather than H I.
