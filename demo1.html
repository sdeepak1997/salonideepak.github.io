Modeling the Lyman Limit

Fit the H I absorption imprinted on a quasar's far-ultraviolet spectrum. The continuum is a Telfer composite scaled to the QSO redshift; each absorber contributes a full Lyman series plus the Lyman-limit edge, with optical depth set by its column density. Everything is convolved with a wavelength-dependent line-spread function before comparison to the data.

Try it on J075547.83+220450.1 (zQSO = 2.3248): adjust the continuum normalization and tilt, add absorbers with a redshift and log N(H I), and click Update model. You can also upload your own spectrum (FITS with wave, flux, err columns) or load a saved results file.
